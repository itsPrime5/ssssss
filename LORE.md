# Field notes recovered from below bedrock

These fragments are deliberately incomplete. Read them in order, or do not.

## I. The drowned plumb line
The first miners lowered a chain until it ran out. The chain came back warm, and every link had turned to face the same direction: down.

## II. The sound under thunder
There is a second thunder, too low to hear. It arrives a little before the first one. Animals stop. Water climbs the banks. The oldest stone gets a hairline crack, then closes it again.

## III. The map with no edges
The cartographer drew the continents correctly. Then she drew the underside. Her map showed a curve so broad that every known shore fit inside one blue fingernail. She burned the map. The ash fell upward.

## IV. The Witness
It has no eyes. The two dim points are places where the crust is thin enough for the deep to look through. It is not approaching. The ground is turning beneath it.

## V. The First Stillness
Do not dig until you find its skin. Do not call the skin stone. Stone is what the skin became after the world forgot how to be alive.
